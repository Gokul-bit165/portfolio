# Brand

We are an elite engineering collective building AI systems with the personality of a creative studio.

The brand should feel:

- Bold
- Editorial
- Technical
- Human
- Unexpected
- Minimal
- Confident

Never look like a generic SaaS company.

Never look corporate.

Never look overly playful.

---

# Visual Direction

Imagine if:

Linear

+

Truus

+

Apple

+

Instrument

+

Framer

designed an AI engineering company.

The experience should feel calm, spacious, premium and memorable.

---

# Layout

Use a broken editorial grid.

Never center every section.

Leave lots of whitespace.

Large headlines.

Floating objects.

Cards overlapping slightly.

Asymmetrical layouts.

Sections should feel curated instead of generated.

---

# Typography

Typography is the hero.

Use four font personalities.

## Display

Syne

Huge.

Bold.

Editorial.

Mostly lowercase or stacked words.

Example

WE BUILD

INTELLIGENT

SYSTEMS

---

## Human Accent

Use Caveat for emphasis.

Examples

actually.

with humans.

not hype.

made to last.

Rotate handwritten text by ±4°.

Underline it.

Circle important words.

Draw arrows toward key content.

Use it sparingly.

---

## Technical

IBM Plex Mono

Use for

navigation

section labels

metadata

project numbers

timestamps

AI models

status badges

Example

EST. 2026

ONLINE

BUILD #014

---

## Body

Inter

Large comfortable reading.

No tiny text.

# Overall Feeling

Visitors should immediately think:

"This team builds exceptional software."

The website should feel like a premium design publication dedicated to AI engineering—clean, spacious, and technically sophisticated, with just enough handcrafted personality through expressive typography and subtle annotations to make it memorable without sacrificing professionalism.
